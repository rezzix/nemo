Why Nemo

One platform. Every perspective.

From sprint-level execution to portfolio-level strategy, Nemo gives every role exactly what they need — no more, no less.

All-in-One Platform

Replace your patchwork of tools. Tasks, time tracking, wiki docs, CRM, leave management, and reporting — natively integrated, no plugins required.

PMO & Portfolio

Built-in Earned Value Management, RAID logs, phase tracking with deliverables and payments, and portfolio-level KPIs across all your projects.

Project Management

Real-time Kanban boards via WebSocket, sprint planning, backlog management, task types & priorities, labels, and drag-and-drop organization.

Enterprise & Governance

Multi-company architecture with fine-grained role-based access. Six distinct roles ensure each user sees only what they should — including restricted external stakeholder access.

Capabilities

Everything your team needs

A comprehensive feature set designed for the entire organization — from individual contributors to the C-suite.

Kanban Boards

Real-time drag-and-drop boards with WebSocket sync across all connected clients.

Sprint Planning

Organize backlogs, create sprints, and track velocity across iterations.

Time Tracking

Log hours per task with weekly and daily timesheet views. Billable rates per user.

EVM & Budget

Earned Value Management with CPI, SPI, CV, SV metrics. Budget vs. actual tracking.

RAID Logs

Track risks, assumptions, issues, and dependencies with probability-impact scoring.

Phases & Deliverables

Define project phases with milestones, deliverable states, and payment tracking.

Wiki & Docs

Nested wiki pages with Markdown, Mermaid diagrams, full-text search, and project scoping.

Client & Pre-Sales CRM

Manage client relationships, contacts, and pre-sales pipeline from opportunity to close.

Reports & Dashboards

Portfolio reports, sprint velocity, workload analysis, time trends, attendance, and headcount.

Leave Management

Request, approve, and track leave with holiday calendars and per-role approval workflows.

Multi-Company

Multi-tenant architecture with company-scoped visibility. Global users manage across organizations.

Role-Based Access

Six distinct roles — Admin, Manager, Executive, HR, Contributor, External — with granular permissions.

Access

Built for every role

Each user gets a tailored experience with the right level of access and visibility.

Admin

System Administrator

  • Full access to all modules and settings
  • User management, companies, audit logs
  • Task types & statuses configuration
  • Organization branding & settings
Manager

Project Manager

  • Create and manage projects & tasks
  • Sprint planning, Kanban configuration
  • PMO dashboards, RAID logs, phases
  • Client & pre-sales management
  • Approve leave requests
Executive

Senior Leadership

  • Portfolio KPIs & budget visibility
  • Company-scoped project overview
  • Risk matrix & EVM metrics
  • View & edit clients & pre-sales
HR

Human Resources

  • People directory & user profiles
  • Leave approval workflows
  • Asset & location tracking
  • Holiday calendar management
  • Attendance & headcount reports
Contributor

Team Member

  • View & update assigned tasks
  • Log time & view personal timesheet
  • Move tasks on Kanban board
  • Add comments & attachments
  • Edit wiki pages
External

External Stakeholder

  • Access to one assigned project only
  • View & edit external-flagged tasks
  • Read project wiki documentation
  • Add comments on external tasks
  • Request & manage own leave
